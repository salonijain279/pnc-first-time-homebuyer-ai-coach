# Trusted AI Coach for First-Time Homebuyers

> CSL Case Competition (PNC Bank case track) · Team UMN: Parul Chaudhary, Bhavisha Chafekar, Saloni Jain, Yehan Wang

A case competition for PNC Bank: design an AI-driven coaching product that helps first-time
homebuyers navigate savings, mortgage rates, and hidden costs with transparent, explainable
guidance instead of generic advice.

## Overview

First-time homebuyers face confusing, high-stakes decisions — shifting mortgage rates, unclear
program eligibility, hidden costs beyond the down payment — with no unbiased guide walking them
through it. The team designed customer personas, mapped the trust factors that make an AI
financial coach credible, and proposed a product concept for PNC built specifically around
explainability rather than a black-box recommendation engine.
